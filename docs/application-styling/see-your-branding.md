# See your organization's logo and name in the app

<span className="badge badge--secondary">Added in 8.4</span>

When an administrator sets your organization's logo and display name, formsflow.ai shows them at the top of the menu. This page explains where to find them and why they might not show.

## Before you start

- You're signed in to formsflow. Any signed-in user sees the logo and name.
- An administrator has saved a logo or display name. See [Set your organization's display name and button style](./set-display-name-and-buttons.md).

## Show the logo and name

1. On a computer, select the menu toggle at the top of the sidebar to expand it.
2. Look at the top of the sidebar: the logo is there, with the display name under it and above the menu.

On a phone or small screen, open the menu. The logo and name appear at the top.

## Check that it worked

You see your organization's logo instead of the formsflow.ai logo, and the display name under it. A long display name shows its first 15 characters followed by "…". Point at it to see the full name.

## Things to know

- The sidebar starts collapsed every time a page loads. The collapsed sidebar shows neither the logo nor the display name.
- The logo and name don't appear on the login screen.
- The logo fits a space of 112 × 48 pixels, aligned left, and is never enlarged.
- If the logo image can't be loaded, the formsflow.ai logo shows instead.
- Changes made by an administrator appear the next time you load a page.
- On a single-tenant installation that uses a custom theme setting `--hide-formsflow-logo: true`, no logo is shown, including an uploaded one.
