# Get help from the AI assistant while filling in a form

<span className="badge badge--secondary">Added in 8.4</span>

:::info Beta
AI-assisted submissions is in beta in formsflow.ai 8.4. Behaviour may change in a later release.
:::

When a formsflow.ai form has an AI assistant, you can ask it what a question means or which rules apply to you, and get an answer from the material the form's owner provided. You submit with fewer doubts, and your submission is less likely to come back for corrections.

## Before you start

- Permission: **Submit › Manage submissions (create, save drafts, resubmit)**
- You're signed in. The assistant isn't available to anonymous users on a public form link.
- The form is published and its designer has turned the assistant on.
- Edition: Go, Professional or Enterprise

## Open the assistant

1. Go to **Submit** and open the form, or open one of your drafts to keep editing it.
2. The assistant opens in the bottom-right corner of the page and shows its welcome text.
3. Read the notice "*Assistant name* is AI and can make mistakes." To read the privacy text, select **Privacy Policy**.
4. Select **I understand**.

To get the assistant out of your way, close it. It collapses to a **Need help?** button; select it to open the assistant again. On a narrow screen, the assistant opens as a panel at the bottom of the screen.

## Ask a question

This works only when the form allows chat. If you see only the welcome text, the form's designer hasn't turned chat on.

1. In **Ask a question...**, type your question. To ask about a field, name the field in your question.
2. Send the question.
3. Wait while the assistant shows **Thinking...**. The answer appears all at once.

Questions can be up to 4,000 characters. The assistant uses up to your last 5 questions and answers as context, so you can ask follow-ups.

## See which documents an answer drew on

Under an answer, the assistant shows **The answers I give are based on these documents.** and a chip for each document it used. Select a chip to download that document.

## Pick up a conversation after reloading the page

Nothing to do: reload the page and the assistant shows your conversation again. You don't need to select **I understand** a second time.

## Check that it worked

After you select **I understand**, the assistant shows the **Ask a question...** box (when chat is allowed). After you ask, your answer appears in place of **Thinking...**.

## Things to know

- The assistant answers questions. It doesn't fill in the form for you, and you still submit the form yourself.
- The assistant isn't available on bundled forms.
- There are no help icons on individual fields. Name the field in the chat instead.
- Answers can be wrong. Check anything important against the source documents.
- Only uploaded documents are listed as sources. Guidance the designer typed into the form is never listed.
- You see no source chips when the form doesn't allow document downloads.
- Your conversation is kept in this browser for this version of the form. In another browser, or after you clear your browser's cache, you start a new conversation.
- There's no way to clear the conversation history.
